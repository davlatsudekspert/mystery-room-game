# Glossary — EN / RU / UZ

The fixed terms of MYSTERY ROOM in the three shipped languages. Every string in
`tools/localization/strings_*.py` uses these forms; a new string should too.
Capitalization and spelling here are authoritative (RU uses ё; UZ is Latin with ʻ U+02BB in oʻ/gʻ and
ʼ U+02BC as the tutuq belgisi).

## UI conventions

| Rule | EN | RU | UZ |
|---|---|---|---|
| Buttons | imperative or noun, Title Case for the label word only (New Game, Continue) | infinitive or noun (Продолжить, Новая игра) | -ish/-ash verbal noun (Davom etish, Birlashtirish, Chiqish) |
| Prompts to the player | "Tap…", "Drag…" | «Нажмите…», «Проведите пальцем…» (formal вы) | «…bosing», «…suring» (formal siz) |
| Leyla's and Strand's notes | plain, first person | ты between Leyla and Strand; «настрой», «не бойся» | sen between Leyla and Strand; siz to the unknown reader |
| Quotes | “ ” (U+201C/D) | « » | « » |
| Dash | — with spaces | — with spaces | — with spaces |
| Ellipsis | … (U+2026) | … | … |
| Ordinals / numbering | Chapter 1, Laboratory 7, Locker 9, Volume II | Глава 1, Лаборатория 7, шкафчик 9, том II | 1-bob, 7-laboratoriya, 9-shkafcha, II-jild |
| Dates | 14 November 1979 | 14 ноября 1979 | 1979-yil 14-noyabr |
| Progress / saves | progress | прогресс | oʻyin (Oʻyin saqlandi, Saqlangan oʻyin) |
| Spelling | British (labelled, centred, coloured, metres, catalogue) | ё always written | oʻ gʻ with ʻ; ʼ in baʼzi, maʼqul |

## What never changes between languages
Codes, counts, orders and positions are shown on the objects and spoken by the level-3 hints through
placeholders: 0-3-1-7, the safe code, 41 m, the beacon numbers and volumes, 4.75, 04 / 1– / 17, locker 9,
dial 285, "longest shadow first", focus mark, "left/right", "a third of a turn", "smallest first".
The symbols ◆ ▲ ● ■ ☼ ☾ ✦ are drawn as art and named in words (`sym.*`).
