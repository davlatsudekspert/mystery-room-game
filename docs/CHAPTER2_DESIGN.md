# Chapter 2: The Missing Scientist (design outline)

**Status:** outline only. Chapter 1 quality comes first. Data hooks already exist in `GameState.profile.choices`.

## Premise
- The door of Lab 7 opens onto the **Records Archive**: long aisles of filing cabinets, a pneumatic-tube mail station, a dark projection booth.
- Leyla's 1998 newspaper clipping proves that she came back to the sealed Institute. **Where did she go?**
- The parcel you received was postmarked 1979.

## New signature mechanics
| Mechanic | Builds on | Idea |
|---|---|---|
| **Light Memory II: overlays** | Ch1 recording | Project two recordings at once. A shadow recorded in one room and a glyph recorded in another combine into a key, and only the overlap opens the vault |
| **Pneumatic post** | Cross-room causality | Send objects between rooms through the tube network. What you send changes what you find in the next room. The tube destinations follow a code printed on the Archive's routing chart |
| **Portable receiver** | Ch1 radio | Leyla's pocket receiver works hot-cold: its signal strength leads you to hidden recorders in the walls. The recordings are Leyla's voice-diary in 1998 |

## Consequences from Chapter 1
| Ch1 result | Effect in Ch2 |
|---|---|
| `ch1_lens = take_lens` | The lens hums near "kept" light. It works as a detector that reveals two optional echoes and a shortcut hint in the Archive |
| `ch1_lens = leave_lens` | Leyla's echo appears once and **points** to the vault mechanism. This is a different solution path for one puzzle with the same difficulty, and the echo is one of the 41 photographs |
| `ch1_shards = 5` | The Archive's projection booth plays a secret reel: Strand's private film, dated 13 Nov 1979 |

## Puzzle sketch (about 12 puzzles, 30–40 min)
1. Archive card catalogue: Dewey-like numbers built from staff ids found on the evidence wall.
2. Pneumatic-tube routing chart. Send the crystal lens to the projection booth.
3. Film splicing: order four frames by the shadows' length, which tracks the time of day.
4. Leyla's 1998 voice-diary on the receiver. Its message is pitch-shifted, so the dial must be tuned to the right playback speed.
5. **Climax:** an overlay of two light recordings opens the Archive vault. Inside is Leyla's 1998 field kit, and a ticket stub that leads to the Underground Facility (Ch3).

## Cliffhanger
Leyla's last 1998 entry reads: "I tried to go in after them." On the projection screen, the 1979 film shows 41 people. A 42nd silhouette stands at the edge of the frame: **Leyla, in 1998**.
