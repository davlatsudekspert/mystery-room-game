# Chapter 2 decals, glyphs, film frames and materials (group F)

All images are **original procedural artwork** (PIL + numpy, no downloads), generated by
`tools/textures/make_decals_ch2.py`. The materials that use them are written by
`tools/materials/make_extra_materials.py`. Puzzle data mirrors `game/src/rooms/archive/archive_logic.gd`.

```
python3 tools/textures/make_decals_ch2.py                  # everything, all languages, both QA sheets (~2.5 min)
python3 tools/textures/make_decals_ch2.py --only badge,index_card --langs ru,uz --no-sheet
python3 tools/textures/make_decals_ch2.py --sheet-only     # qa/decals_ch2_contact_sheet.jpg + qa/decals_ch2_lang_sheet.jpg
python3 tools/materials/make_extra_materials.py            # rewrites the .tres (Chapter 1 files come out byte-identical)
```

The output goes to `game/assets/textures/decals/ch2/` and `game/assets/textures/linoleum/`. The QA sheet is
`qa/decals_ch2_contact_sheet.jpg`.

## Conventions

- **Decal UVs** follow `docs/models/ch2.md` §0: u runs left → right and v bottom → top as the player sees it.
  The image is upright, as shown on the contact sheet. Pixel coordinates below are image pixels with y running
  **down** from the top edge, so v = 1 − y / height.
- **Glyphs and the engraving** draw only in **ALPHA**. Their RGB is pure white. The shaders read `.a`.
- **RGBA decals** use alpha for real cut-outs: notches, sprocket holes, spindle holes and disc outlines. Their
  materials use alpha scissor (0.5). RGB under alpha 0 is a dark "hole" colour, so the image still reads
  correctly if a material ignores alpha.
- **Photos and film frames** are RGB JPG.
- **Languages:** every decal with readable words exists as `<name>.<ext>` (EN), `<name>_ru.<ext>` and
  `<name>_uz.<ext>` (see "Languages" below). Everything a puzzle needs is a numeral or a pictogram, identical in
  every language: 0417, the notches, 4.75, shadow lengths, symbols and the shelf code B-3.

### Linoleum texture set (`game/assets/textures/linoleum/`)

`albedo.jpg`, `normal.png` (OpenGL Y+, as `fetch_textures.py`) and `orm.jpg` (R = AO, G = roughness, B = metal 0) are
1024² each. One repeat is a **2 × 2 checker of 0.30 m tiles** (dark green / cream, 512 px per tile), and the
image edges are tile joints, so it **tiles seamlessly** (checked on a 2 × 2 repeat).

- **Tiles:** each tile has a directional marbled chip pattern, with the grain quarter-turned between green and
  cream, and a slight dye-lot difference between the two tiles of each colour.
- **Wear:** dirt in the joints, wax build-up haze, a faded and duller walkway, clusters of black heel marks,
  fine scratches and chipped corners showing dark adhesive.
- **Roughness:** waxed ≈ 0.34 (green) and 0.40 (cream), up to ≈ 0.55 in the walkway; scuffs, scratches and joints
  are rougher.
- **Normal:** the joint groove, slight tile-edge curl, scratches and chips.

## Languages (EN / RU / UZ)

No untranslated words are baked into any image.

- **Variant files:** 9 decals carry words and get variants, 18 files in all:
  - `badge`, `index_card`, `request_card`, `routing_chart`, `file_cover`: `.png`;
  - `archive_rules`: `.jpg`;
  - `tape_label_1996/1997/1998`: `.png`.

  Each has `<name>_ru.<ext>` and `<name>_uz.<ext>` next to the EN file in `game/assets/textures/decals/ch2/`.
  `DecalLoc` swaps the albedo texture by language; the `.tres` files keep pointing at the EN file.
- **No variants:** these have no words: glyphs, `vault_engraving`, `dest_symbols`, film frames, `film_secret`,
  `vault_reel`, linoleum, `reel_can_lid` ("E.S. 1979" is initials), `slide_mark` ("E.S. 1971"), `film_strip_0..3`
  and `box_labels`. The last two were made word-free for this: key-code edge marks and number codes.
- **Text table:** `TXT` and `RULES_TXT` in the script. The terminology follows `game/localization/strings.csv`:
  - archive name: Архив «Б» / «B» arxivi;
  - пневмопочта / pnevmatik pochta, Табельный № / Xodim №, хранилище / seyfxona;
  - Leyla's initials: RU Л.Р., as in `doc2.file`; UZ keeps L.R.;
  - Strand: Э. Странд;
  - institute: ИНСТИТУТ «МЕРИДИАН» / «MERIDIAN» INSTITUTI;
  - department: Отдел физики света / Yorugʻlik fizikasi boʻlimi.
- **Fixed in every language:** the shelf code **B-3** (matches the 3D range label of the stacks) and the speed
  **4.75** (matches the deck's selector).
- **Fitting:** long RU/UZ lines shrink to their slot. `Pen.text(..., max_w=)`, `typed(..., max_w=)` and
  `arc_text(..., max_deg=)` handle single lines; the rules list shrinks its type until it clears the stamp.
- **Glyphs:**
  - Every font used covers Cyrillic.
  - The URW fonts and Caveat lack **ʻ (U+02BB)**. `_runs()` draws that one glyph from a covering font of the same
    family: FreeSans / FreeSerif (GNU FreeFont is derived from the URW Nimbus fonts), Liberation Mono for the
    typewriter (FreeMono's ʻ looks like an acute accent), and Inter Bold inside URW Gothic titles.
  - The UZ handwritten lines contain no ʻ.
  - A character that no font covers raises an error, so a missing-glyph box cannot ship. The run prints the
    substitutions it made.
- **QA:** `qa/decals_ch2_lang_sheet.jpg` shows EN | RU | UZ side by side. Each variant was inspected at full size;
  the alpha channels (notches, holes, outlines) are identical across languages.

## Notes for the lead and other groups

1. **Shader rotation sense: fixed** in commit fae764e. `vault_overlay.gdshader` and `screen_projection.gdshader`
   now turn images clockwise, as the uniforms are documented. Before the fix, the right-port sign at the solution
   state (rot 2, zoom 3) overlapped the engraving with IoU 0.06, matching it only at rot 6. With the corrected
   formula the simulated IoU is 0.98.
2. **Index card rod hole.** The bottom-centre catalogue hole is cut by alpha scissor in `card_face` only: Ø 6 mm
   52 px above the bottom edge on the centre line, which is Blender card-local (x, y) = (0, −H/2 + 0.0052). The card body underneath is visible
   only from below. The item builder may punch a matching hole through the body, or ignore it.
3. **Strand's mark proportions.** The ring outer diameter is 0.80 of the image, per the contract, so the meridian
   and ticks are slightly shorter relative to the ring than on the Chapter 1 wall emblem (which has the ring at
   0.625). The design is the same: ring, meridian and 12 tick positions.
4. **Routing chart.** It follows the contract's 5-row table, which is newer than the design doc's "request card
   → 📖 only". Only the request-card row matters for the puzzle; the others are dressing that make the chart
   plausible.
5. **Film strips.** All three frames of each strip show the same obelisk shot; the contract requires it only for
   the middle frame. Every frame of a strip has the same shadow, so there is no ambiguity.
6. **Brightness.** The cinema screen shader multiplies the film image by 1.45. The frames are graded with
   midtones ≈ 0.3–0.5, so highlights bloom and shadows stay dark on screen.
7. **New texture sets.** Only `linoleum` is new. `M_Paint_Green`, `M_Concrete`, `M_Steel_Cream` and `M_Cardboard`
   reuse the existing CC0 sets with tints computed from the texture means (numbers above).
8. **Not yet imported.** The new images, including the 18 `_ru` / `_uz` variants, have no `.import` files yet: run `godot --headless --path game --import`.
