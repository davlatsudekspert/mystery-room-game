Decades later, a parcel arrives for you with an old lab badge and a single line: "Laboratory 7. Please finish what I could not."

- Hand-crafted 3D rooms full of tactile brass mechanisms
- Fair, logical puzzles with a three-step hint system and no random guessing
- Signature Lumen mechanics: record light in a crystal and project it as a key, and steer a living beam with mirrors
- UV ink, radio beacons, electrical circuits, shadows and hidden rooms
- A haunting original story told through objects, handwriting and light
- Choices that carry into later chapters
- Fully playable in English, Russian and Uzbek, and offline
- The first two chapters are free. One fair purchase unlocks the rest. No ads, no energy, no subscriptions.
