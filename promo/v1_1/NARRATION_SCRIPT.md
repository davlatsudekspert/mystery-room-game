# Mystery Room: Lost Institute 1.1 - narration script

English is spoken (offline neural voice, piper `en_GB-cori-high`, slow and calm, see README.md). Russian and Uzbek exist only as burned-in
subtitles and `.srt` files; the picture, the voice and the music are the same in all three versions. Times are in the final files
(`timeline.json`). Short lines on purpose: one idea per line, no spoilers, no solutions.

* "Ten new puzzles" and "seven rooms / three floors" are the counts of `docs/CHAPTER2_EXPANSION.md` (N1-N10; the hall plus six new rooms).
